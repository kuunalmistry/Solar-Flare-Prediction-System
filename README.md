☀️ Solar Flare Prediction

A machine learning project that predicts the occurrence and intensity of solar flares using historical solar activity data. The objective is to assist in space weather forecasting by identifying patterns that can indicate future solar flare events, enabling earlier awareness of potential impacts on satellites, communication systems, GPS navigation, and power infrastructure.

⸻

Overview

Solar flares are sudden bursts of energy released from the Sun that can significantly affect Earth’s technological systems. This project leverages machine learning techniques to analyze historical solar observations and predict future flare activity.

The system performs both:

* Solar Flare Occurrence Prediction – Determines whether a solar flare is likely to occur.
* Solar Flare Intensity Classification – Predicts the expected severity or class of the solar flare.
