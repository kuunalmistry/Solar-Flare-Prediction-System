A. Project Reorientation & Revised Plan

Refined Problem Statement

Solar flares are sudden bursts of electromagnetic radiation from the Sun that can severely disrupt satellite communications, GPS, aviation, and power grids. Current forecasting relies heavily on manual expert analysis, which is time-consuming, subjective, and reactive. Goal: Develop a machine learning model that automatically predicts solar flares (binary and multi-class), using NASA and NOAA solar activity data, to enable faster and more reliable early warnings.

Quick facts
  • Dataset: solar flares events catalog
  • Models trained: RandomForest, HistGradientBoosting
  • Prediction: flare class / event severity
